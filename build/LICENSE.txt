Bench.

Made at TomFit Health Training AG by Noel Jensen, for internal use.

This program runs on your machine. It stores its board in a folder you choose
and your own settings, sign-in tokens and hours in your Windows user profile.
It talks only to Microsoft 365 (Planner, your OneDrive workbook) and the
TomFit tools you sign in to. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
